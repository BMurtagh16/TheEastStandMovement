More on how this is made

Scores, lineups and substitution minutes come from football-data.org and are verifiable. The marks, the manager card and the mood index are produced by a language model reading published journalism and applying the same rubric every week. There is no tracking data and no xG feed. Any card that could not be checked against the results feed is labelled unverified.

Stories are summarised with attribution and a link, never republished. Club names and trademarks belong to their owners, and nothing here is endorsed by or connected to any club.
