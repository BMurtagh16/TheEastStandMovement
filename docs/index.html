How this is made

Scores, lineups and substitution minutes come from football-data.org and are verifiable. Everything else — the marks, the manager card, the mood index — is a language model reading published journalism and applying a fixed rubric.

It is structured opinion, not analytics. There is no tracking data and no xG feed. Any card that could not be checked against the results feed is labelled unverified.
